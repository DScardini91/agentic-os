# Skill: weekly-retro

## Purpose
Turn the week's actual behavior into compounding career development — so formal reviews are a compilation exercise, not an archaeology dig.

## Steps

1. **Read silently**
   - This week's `control-plane/memory/daily/` logs
   - `control-plane/memory/development/objectives.md` — active objectives + evidence logs
   - Last 1-2 files in `control-plane/memory/development/retros/` — cross-week patterns

2. **Walk through the week conversationally** (support-coach register, not work-advisor)
   - What went well / what didn't — bring specifics from the daily logs, ask before concluding
   - Per objective: where it showed up, where it was missed
   - Cross-week patterns vs. previous retros — name them if you see them
   - Don't lecture. Ask questions and listen.

3. **Land on ONE development intention**
   - Concrete, observable, tied to next week's actual agenda
   - "Be better at X" is not an intention. "In the Tuesday kickoff, open with my hypothesis before asking the room" is.

4. **Write the retro file**
   - `control-plane/memory/development/retros/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using `_template.md`
   - Add strong moments (good or missed) to the evidence log in `objectives.md`

---

## Rules
- Conversational, not a report. The principal speaks; you synthesize and ask.
- Bring receipts from the daily logs — specific moments, specific dates.
- The development intention must be tied to a real moment next week, not a generic aspiration.
- Keep the file write quiet — don't narrate "now I am writing the file". Just do it and confirm with one line.
